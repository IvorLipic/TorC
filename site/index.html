TorC · backprop Lab GitHub

Backpropagation, one step at a time

Step through a small computation forward, then backward, and see exactly what is multiplied by what, with the real numbers computed by TorC, a from-scratch C++ tensor library.

Three ideas are enough

Every value and gradient shown for the preset inputs was printed by the real TorC library (visualizations/examples/autograd_trace.cpp) and cross-checked against finite differences; edited inputs are recomputed in your browser by a small JavaScript port of the same rules, which is tested against the C++ output. Source: github.com/IvorLipic/TorC.
